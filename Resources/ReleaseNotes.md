**Live mode** - 18 August 2026
One switch for whether the file and the page keep in step on their own.
Off, Kvill is an ordinary editor: Cmd S saves, Cmd R reloads.

**Changes are marked** - 18 August 2026
When another program writes the file, the words it changed light up for a
moment and then fade. Useful when an agent is editing alongside you.

**Folder sidebar** - 17 August 2026
Open a folder and its Markdown files sit down the side of the window.
A set of notes is one window now, rather than one window each.

**Six colour schemes** - 17 August 2026
The translucent ones are gone and the rest were reworked to match.
Control-Command-] steps through them.

**Open files faster** - 14 August 2026
Kvill can wait in the background so the next document costs a window
rather than a whole launch. Off unless you ask for it.

**Typefaces, sizes and widths** - 14 August 2026
Four typographic settings, shown as specimens rather than named in a list.
Command-plus and Command-minus change the size from anywhere.

**Focus mode** - 14 August 2026
Everything but the paragraph you are in fades back.
Command-Shift-F, and Command-period hides every piece of interface.

**Typewriter scrolling** - 14 August 2026
Keeps the line you are typing at the middle of the window instead of
letting it walk to the bottom edge.

**The slash menu** - 14 August 2026
Press / on an empty line for headings, tables, code blocks, callouts and
lists, without remembering any of the syntax.

**Syntax markers that get out of the way** - 14 August 2026
Markers appear in the margin for the element the caret is in, and fade
again when you leave. The file on disk never changes.
