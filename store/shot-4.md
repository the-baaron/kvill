# Set properly, not just styled

Eight colour palettes and five typefaces, each with the line height, the measure and the spacing that suit it. Four text sizes and three column widths.

## Read at a distance

Focus mode dims everything but the paragraph you are in, so a long document stops shouting all at once.

> A blockquote keeps its bar in the margin, and quiet text stays quiet.

Typewriter scrolling keeps the line you are writing at eye level rather than at the bottom edge of the window, which is where a text view would otherwise leave it.

Every one of these is remembered across the whole app, not per document.
